Get DJ on your Mac

Paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link and DJ saves it as a top-quality MP3 with the cover art and artist name already filled in, ready for rekordbox, Serato, djay or Mixxx.

Download DJ macOS 13 or newer · any Mac · 1 MB

Install it

  1. 01

    Unzip it and move it to Applications

    Open your Downloads folder and double-click DJ.zip (Safari often does this for you). Drag DJ into Applications.

  2. 02

    Open DJ, then click Done first time only

    Your Mac will show a warning. That's normal for apps that don't come from the App Store. Click Done.

    You'll see something like Apple could not verify “DJ” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
    Move to BinDone
  3. 03

    Allow it in System Settings first time only

    Open System Settings (the grey gear app) and click Privacy & Security in the sidebar. Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to the DJ message.

    Privacy & Security › Security
    “DJ” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway

    Your Mac asks for your password or Touch ID, then shows one more box. Click Open Anyway there too.

  4. 04

    Let it set itself up

    The first time, DJ spends about a minute downloading what it needs. You'll see a progress bar. After that it opens straight away.

Downloading a song

Copy

Copy a link from YouTube or SoundCloud.

Open DJ

The link is already filled in. Click Download.

Done

Click Show in Finder. Songs live in Music › DJ.

Playlist links download the whole playlist. In your DJ software, add the Music › DJ folder once and new songs show up there.

If something goes wrong

It says “Download failed”
Check the link opens in your browser, then quit DJ and open it again. DJ updates itself once a week, and YouTube changes often, so a fresh start usually fixes it. If not, message whoever sent you this page.
I can't find “Open Anyway”
Open DJ once first (step 02). The button only appears in Privacy & Security for about an hour after your Mac blocks it.
Removing DJ
Drag DJ from Applications to the Bin. To remove its tools too, in Finder press ⇧⌘G, type ~/.dj and delete that folder. Your songs stay where they are.
